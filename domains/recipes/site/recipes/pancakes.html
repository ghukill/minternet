Fluffy Pancakes

These classic pancakes are light, fluffy, and perfect for a lazy weekend breakfast. The secret is not overmixing the batter - a few lumps are fine!

Prep Time 10 minutes
Cook Time 10 minutes
Servings 4 (about 12 pancakes)

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, beat the egg, then add the milk, melted butter, and vanilla. Mix well.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir gently until just combined - the batter should be slightly lumpy.
  4. Heat a griddle or non-stick pan over medium heat. Lightly grease with butter.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set, about 2-3 minutes.
  7. Flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Serve immediately with maple syrup, fresh fruit, or your favorite toppings.

Tips

  • Let the batter rest for 5 minutes before cooking for extra fluffy pancakes.
  • Don't press down on the pancakes while cooking - this deflates them.
  • Keep cooked pancakes warm in a 200°F oven while making the rest.
