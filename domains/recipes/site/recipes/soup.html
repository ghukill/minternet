Hearty Vegetable Soup

This warming soup is packed with nutritious vegetables and makes a satisfying meal on its own. Feel free to substitute whatever vegetables you have on hand.

Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 30 minutes
Servings 6

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup green beans, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add carrots, celery, and potatoes. Stir to combine.
  5. Pour in the diced tomatoes and vegetable broth.
  6. Add thyme and oregano. Bring to a boil.
  7. Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
  8. Add green beans and corn. Continue simmering for 10 more minutes until all vegetables are tender.
  9. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  10. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley.

Variations

  • Add protein: Stir in cooked beans, lentils, or shredded chicken.
  • Make it creamy: Blend half the soup and stir it back in.
  • Pasta soup: Add small pasta shapes in the last 10 minutes.
  • Spicy version: Add red pepper flakes or diced jalapeño.
