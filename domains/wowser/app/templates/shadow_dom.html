Why Shadow DOM Is Challenging

  • Content is not in the initial HTML
  • Shadow roots are created via JavaScript
  • Styles are encapsulated (can't access from outside)
  • Traditional HTML capture misses shadow content

Capture Strategies

  • Use browser-based capture (Browsertrix, etc.)
  • Wait for JavaScript to execute before capture
  • Serialize shadow DOM during capture
  • Some tools can "pierce" shadow boundaries
